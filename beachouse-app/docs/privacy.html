Privacy policy

Beachouse Health Club app · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the Beachouse Health Club app ("the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is run by Beachouse, Bondi Beach, Sydney, Australia. Questions: support@beachousebondi.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, first name, and whether you opted in to news and offers. Only if you create an account.
  • Training and recovery data you enter: sets, weights, reps, cardio, bathhouse sessions, body weight, goals, focus, injuries to work around, and anything you log in the Health section (results and food).
  • Coach questions: if you message the in-app coach, your message and the training context needed to answer it.
  • Feedback: anything you send through the Feedback tab or survey, with your rating and email if you give it.
  • App usage: basic events such as screens opened and links tapped, and how you found the app (for example a search or ad), so we can see what is working.

Photos and the camera are used only on your phone, to scan machine QR codes and make story images. We do not upload your photos.

How we use it

  • To run the app and save your progress to your account so it is safe on any device.
  • To answer coach questions and personalise your sessions.
  • To send account emails such as confirming your email and resetting your password.
  • To send news and offers, only if you opted in. You can opt out any time.
  • To understand and improve the app and our marketing.

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not track you across other companies' apps.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (secure database and logins, hosted in Sydney, Australia).
  • Resend (account emails).
  • Anthropic (generates coach replies; messages are not used to train its models).
  • Cloudflare and GitHub (hosting).

Health information

Health and fitness information you enter is private to your account and protected so that only you can read it. It is not used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

Without an account, your data stays only on your phone. With an account, we keep it until you delete your account. You can delete your account and everything saved to it at any time in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by emailing us.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act you can ask to access or correct your information, or make a complaint, by emailing support@beachousebondi.com. If you are not happy with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
