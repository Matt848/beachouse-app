Common questions

  • Forgot your password? On the log in screen tap "Forgot password?" and we will email you a reset link.
  • Didn't get the confirmation email? Check your junk folder, then try logging in again.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account and all saved data.
  • Gym and bathhouse bookings: visit beachousebondi.com.
